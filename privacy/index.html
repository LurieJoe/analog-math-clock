Privacy Policy

Analog Math Clock is designed so that your clocks, equations, alarms, and pictures stay under your control.

The short version: Analog Math Clock has no accounts, advertising, behavioral analytics, or developer-operated server database. Your clocks and pictures stay in local browser storage unless you choose to export or share them.

Information stored by the app

Analog Math Clock stores clock definitions, time zones, appearance preferences, custom equations, alarms, and app settings in your browser's local storage. Uploaded clock-face pictures are stored in the browser's IndexedDB storage. This information remains associated with the browser and website address on the device where you use the app.

Information the app does not collect

  • No account, username, password, or sign-in information.
  • No advertising identifier or behavioral tracking.
  • No application analytics about your clocks, equations, alarms, or activity.
  • No cloud copy of your clocks or pictures maintained by the app developer.

Pictures and local processing

Clock-face pictures selected from your device are read and stored locally by your browser. Positioning, resizing, overlays, clock rendering, equation generation, time calculations, and image exports are performed on your device. Analog Math Clock does not upload pictures to an application server.

Alarms and notifications

Alarm schedules are stored locally. If you request notification permission, your browser or operating system manages that permission and delivery. Analog Math Clock does not operate a remote notification service, and background notification delivery cannot be guaranteed. System notifications may be visible on a lock screen or to another person using your device. The app hides alarm and clock names by default; you can change this setting in Tools.

Exports, sharing, and backups

PNG and SVG exports and .mathclock backups are created locally. A file leaves the app only when you choose a destination through your browser or operating system. A full backup may contain every clock, custom equation, alarm, preference, and uploaded picture stored by Analog Math Clock, so keep backup files private.

Website hosting

The public website and application files are delivered through GitHub Pages. GitHub and the network services used to reach the site may process standard request information such as an IP address, requested URL, browser details, and timestamps under their own policies. Analog Math Clock does not add application analytics or tracking scripts.

External links and feedback

Links may open GitHub or Flashcard Flurry. Those sites receive standard web request information under their own policies. GitHub issues, comments, screenshots, device details, and browser details are public. Do not include personal information or attach a .mathclock backup or private clock-face picture to a GitHub issue.

Security and retention

Local app data remains in your browser until you remove it, clear site data, or uninstall the app. Browser storage is not a substitute for device encryption or access controls; someone who can use your unlocked device or browser profile may be able to view the app's clocks, alarm labels, equations, and pictures.

Deleting your information

You can remove individual clocks, pictures, custom equations, and alarms from the app. Removing Analog Math Clock site data from your browser deletes locally stored app data in that browser. Before clearing browser storage or uninstalling, export a full backup if you may need the data later.

Changes to this policy

This policy may be updated when the app's behavior or supporting services change. The revision date at the top identifies the current version.

Questions

Analog Math Clock is operated by Joe Lurie. Send private privacy, security, or support questions to contact@analogmathclock.com. Use GitHub only for information you are comfortable posting publicly.

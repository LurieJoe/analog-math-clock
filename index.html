Help Center

Quick answers about storage, installation, backups, alarms, and updates.

Where is my Analog Math Clock data stored?

Clocks, settings, equations, and alarms stay in this browser. Uploaded face pictures are stored locally on this device.

How do I move my clocks to another device?

Use Export backup, save the .mathclock file, and restore it on the other device. The backup includes uploaded face pictures.

Will alarms work when the app is closed?

Alarms are reliable while Analog Math Clock is open. Mobile operating systems may suspend closed apps, so background notifications are best-effort.
